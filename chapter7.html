CHAPTER 7: MULTIMEDIA

7.1 Introduction to Multimedia

Multimedia is the integration of multiple media formats—such as text, graphics, audio, video, and animation—processed, stored, and presented digitally to communicate information interactively.

Linear Multimedia: Content progresses without user navigation control (e.g., a movie, television broadcast).
Non-Linear (Interactive) Multimedia: Content allows user navigation, input, and interaction (e.g., video games, educational software).

7.2 Components of Multimedia

Text: The primary medium used to convey basic information, titles, and instructions clearly.
• Common Formats: .txt, .docx, .pdf

Graphics: Digital visual images, photos, drawings, and diagrams used to illustrate concepts visually.
• Common Formats: .jpg, .png, .gif, .svg

Audio: Sound elements including speech, voiceovers, sound effects, and background music.
• Common Formats: .mp3, .wav, .aac

Video: Moving visual images recorded real-time or generated digitally, often combined with synced audio.
• Common Formats: .mp4, .avi, .mkv

Animation: Sequential display of static images or drawings generated rapidly to create the illusion of motion.
• Common Formats: .gif, .swf, .mp4

7.3 Applications of Multimedia

Education & E-Learning: Interactive digital classrooms, animated educational tutorials, and digital textbooks.
Entertainment & Gaming: 3D video games, animated movies, and streaming platforms (e.g., YouTube, Netflix).
Business & Advertising: Interactive promotional presentations, digital marketing banners, and video advertisements.
Healthcare & Medical Science: 3D medical surgical simulations, diagnostic imaging visualization, and virtual anatomy models.
Virtual Reality & Simulation: Flight simulators for pilot training and immersive VR environments for architectural walkthroughs.
